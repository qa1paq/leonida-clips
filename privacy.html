Leonida Clips Publisher – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Leonida Clips Publisher ("the app") is a personal desktop tool operated by the owner of the Leonida Clips channels (@leonidaclipspot). It uploads short, credited highlight clips of GTA 6 live streams, edited by the owner under the streamers' official clipping programs, to the owner's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.

What data the app uses

  • OAuth access tokens issued by YouTube, Instagram/Facebook and TikTok for the owner's own accounts.
  • Basic account information returned by these platforms (account ID and username), used only to publish videos.

How data is stored

All tokens and settings are stored only on the owner's personal computer. The app has no server, does not collect data about other people, does not sell or share data with third parties and does not use data for advertising.

Data deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the security settings of each platform. Deleting the app's local secrets folder removes all stored tokens.
